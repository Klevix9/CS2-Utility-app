# Store listing

**App name:** CS2 Utility: Lineups

**Short description (80 characters max):**
Tap where you want a smoke, flash, molly or HE on Dust II and see where to throw.

**Full description:**

Plan your utility between rounds. Pick a grenade, tap the spot you want it to land on the Dust II map, and choose where to throw it from. Each lineup tells you where to stand, where to aim, and how to throw: standing or crouching, still, jump throw or run throw, and left or right click.

• Every landing spot for the chosen grenade is marked on the map
• Mark where you are and spots you can't reach fade out
• Step-by-step throw instructions with optional aim screenshots
• Record your own lineups: paste the output of getpos for an exact spot and angle
• Share lineups with your team by exporting and importing a file
• Works offline, no account, no ads

CS2 Utility is an unofficial fan-made tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve Corporation. Counter-Strike is a trademark of Valve Corporation.
